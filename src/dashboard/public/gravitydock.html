How It Works

Three steps to flying apps

01

Pick Your Apps

Choose which apps fly from the settings panel. Your Dock apps are auto-selected. Add or remove anything at any time.

02

Choose a Mode

Bounce off walls, drift in zero gravity, fall with real gravity, or go full chaos. Adjust speed and icon size in settings.

03

Click to Launch

Transparent floating panels appear over your screen. Click any flying icon to instantly open that app — fully functional.

Physics Engine

4 Physics Modes

Switch modes in real time without restarting

Bounce

Icons ricochet off screen edges like rubber balls. Classic elastic physics.

Float

Icons drift peacefully in zero gravity. Wraps screen edges for a zen desktop.

Gravity

Real downward pull. Icons fall and pile up bouncing on the screen floor.

Chaos

Random forces push icons in every direction. Pure kinetic energy on screen.

Installation

Running in under 60 seconds

Quick Install

# Open the DMG and drag to Applications
$ open GravityDock.dmg
$ cp -R /Volumes/GravityDock/GravityDock.app /Applications/

# First launch — right-click the app icon and choose Open

Fix: "App is damaged" warning

$ xattr -cr /Applications/GravityDock.app
$ codesign --force --deep --sign - /Applications/GravityDock.app

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Universal binary — runs on Apple Silicon and Intel.
