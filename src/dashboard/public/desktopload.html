What It Does

Real-Time Gauge

A circular pressure gauge shows your desktop load from 0 to 10 GB. Green means clean, red means cluttered. Updates automatically every 20 seconds.

Category Breakdown

See exactly what's eating your space — Images, Videos, Documents, Archives, Audio, Apps, and Folders. Each category gets its own color-coded bar.

One-Click Cleanup

Move old files to trash based on age. Or archive everything into a dated folder. Both actions are safe and reversible.

Heaviest Items List

See the top 8 biggest files and folders ranked by size. Know exactly what to delete first for maximum impact.

Smart Tips

Get contextual cleanup tips based on your current load. Learn to spot screenshots, duplicate files, and leftover installers.

Menu Bar + Window

Full window app with sidebar navigation plus a menu bar icon for quick checks. Three tabs: Dashboard, Files, and Cleanup tools.
