Overview

What we're building

A premium booking platform that feels like a travel journal, not a SaaS dashboard. Browsing is discovery-led, the visual identity is sepia-warm and boutique, and every screen — from search to checkout to host dashboard — is designed to feel calm and human.

Scope

Features included in the launch

Every item below ships with Phase 1 (web live by June 30) and Phase 2 (iOS & Android live on the App Store + Google Play by July 15). New requests during Phase 3 are added during the maintenance retainer.

★

Best Offers system

Hosts request featured slots, admin approves, listings surface in a "Best Offers" filter for guests.

⌖

Managed Locations

Curated villages (Marakia, Hacienda, etc.) labelled as platform-managed for guests' peace of mind.

✦

Social Sign-In

Sign in with Google + Apple (iCloud). Frictionless onboarding on every platform.

⌥

Identity verification (KYC)

Mandatory verification for hosts and guests — ID front, ID back, selfie. Reviewed by admin.

#

Reservation codes

Short, human-readable codes per reservation. Searchable in admin and host dashboards.

⌘

Enhanced location entry

Hosts paste a Google Maps link — system auto-fills coordinates. Address autocomplete on the map.

⊞

Home vs. Service split

Listings split into Home (villa, apartment, chalet) and Service (yacht, event, beach buggy) at creation.

$

Locked pricing on booked dates

Hosts can't modify the price for dates that already have a confirmed reservation.

⌗

Listing wizard reorder

Streamlined creation flow with clearer step order. Faster host onboarding.

A

Bilingual EN / AR

Full English + Arabic with proper RTL flip on every screen. Translation keys synchronised across builds.

◎

AI travel assistant

Built-in chat powered by Gemini for travel suggestions and booking help.

◐

Real-time bookings

Live availability, automated booking timeouts, instant push notifications to hosts.

Terms & conditions

  • Scope. Features listed under "Features included in the launch" are committed for Phase 1 and Phase 2. Net-new features requested after sign-off enter the Phase 3 maintenance backlog.
  • UI / UX design included. The studio delivers the full visual design system — colour palette, typography, components, screens, and high-fidelity mockups for web, iOS and Android — as part of the EGP 60,000 quotation. No separate design fee.
  • Deadlines. Web platform live by June 30, 2026. iOS & Android live on the App Store and Google Play by July 15, 2026 — fully published, not internal testing tracks. Delays caused by missing client assets (API keys, content, identity documents, brand approvals) or by store-review hold-ups outside the studio's control extend the deadline by the equivalent time lost.
  • Scope of the EGP 60,000. This quotation covers the studio's development, design and delivery work only. It is exclusive of any third-party fees — including (but not limited to) cloud hosting / infrastructure (AWS, Vercel, Supabase paid tiers, etc.), payment-provider fees (Paymob's 3% + EGP 1 per transaction), Apple Developer and Google Play store fees, SMS / email providers, and any other vendor a guest or host transacts with. Those are billed by the providers directly and stay outside this contract.
  • Maintenance retainer. The final two instalments cover up to 30 hours per month of development time, including bug fixes, small feature additions, performance work and host support.
  • In-app payments. Paymob gateway integrated at launch — supports Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, InstaPay, WE Pay, Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash and Etisalat Cash. Paymob's transaction fee is 3% + EGP 1 per successful transaction, deducted by Paymob from each payout (not by us).
  • Source code. Full source code is delivered to the client's GitHub or GitLab organisation at the end of Phase 2. The client owns the code, the design files and the brand.
  • Payment. Transfers via Instapay, WE Pay or bank transfer to Problem-X. First instalment is due on signing; subsequent instalments at each milestone described above.
  • Confidentiality. This quotation, pricing and any technical details are confidential between Problem-X and the QuickIn team for the validity period above.
