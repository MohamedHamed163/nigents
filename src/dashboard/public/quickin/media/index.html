3. What We Deliver Every Month

The same output repeats each month (July, August, September):

  • Calendar: 1 full content calendar, mapped across Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.
  • Posts: 8 social media posts (carousel or static).
  • Stories: 5 story concepts (standalone or supporting the posts).
  • Scripts: 2–4 video scripts (teasers, reels, promos) — then produced into finished videos.
  • Production: 2 North Coast shoots per month — units + day-in-the-life + model reels.
  • Edited assets: Edited reels, photo sets, and post graphics from each shoot.
  • Publishing: Posting across all 3 platforms every week.
  • Review: End-of-month performance review with the media buyer.

4. Media Production Plan (North Coast)

The production team travels to the North Coast twice each month. Each trip feeds about two weeks of content.

Before the trip
  • Shot list per unit and a storyboard for each reel.
  • Coordinate the model, access to units, and gear.
During the trip
  • Shoot each unit — photos + video (rooms, pool, view, details).
  • Capture day-in-the-life lifestyle footage (morning coffee, beach, sunset).
  • Film model reels and any trend-based clips.
After the trip
  • Back up all footage into the shared content bank.
  • Editor cuts reels/teasers; designer builds post and story creatives.
  • Content creator writes captions (AR/EN) and schedules across IG, FB, TikTok.

5. Content Mix & Angles

Every month's calendar follows the same mix (from the strategy):

  • 40% Pain points — the broker/Facebook problems QuickIn solves.
  • 40% Destinations — Sahel, El Gouna, Ain Sokhna, Hurghada.
  • 20% App demo — how easy and safe booking is.

Lead angles to rotate: Safety, Trust, Convenience, Availability, and Local Experience.

Go-to video ideas
  • Problem / Solution (broker pain → QuickIn).
  • POV booking journey.
  • Questions people always ask.
  • Behind-the-scenes from the shoots + model lifestyle reels.

7. Simple Monthly Rhythm (4 weeks)

  • Week 1: Plan the month's calendar + Shoot Trip 1.
  • Week 2: Edit and publish batch 1 across IG, FB, TikTok.
  • Week 3: Shoot Trip 2 + keep publishing.
  • Week 4: Edit and publish batch 2 + review results with the media buyer.
