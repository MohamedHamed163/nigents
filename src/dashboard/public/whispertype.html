How it works

Four steps from voice to structured plan. Each step takes a second, the whole loop is under five.

1

Speak · تحدث

Hit the orb and talk in English or Arabic. Whisper auto-detects your language.

2

Transcribe · تفريغ

OpenAI Whisper converts voice to accurate text in seconds, handling natural speech in both languages.

3

Structure · تنظيم

GPT-4o-mini breaks it into steps with titles, descriptions, minutes, and priority — in your language.

4

Act · نفذ

See a prioritised, time-estimated plan. Every whisper is saved — browse history anytime.

Install

Requires an OpenAI API key. Create one at platform.openai.com/api-keys — WhisperType calls whisper-1 for transcription and gpt-4o-mini for structuring. Typical cost per whisper: under 1 US cent.

Open the disk image, drag WhisperType.app into Applications, then eject the volume.

  • If Gatekeeper blocks: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open anyway.
  • If needed: xattr -cr ~/Downloads/WhisperType-1.0.0.dmg then open again.
  • On first launch, paste your OpenAI API key — stored locally in the macOS Keychain.
  • Grant microphone permission when prompted.
