Workspace — Home services & construction

example.com
example.com — live preview

About

Workspace is a home-services & construction marketplace for the Gulf market. Homeowners post a project — a bathroom renovation, an AC install, a paint job, plumbing or tile work, anything from a small repair to a full apartment refit — and verified service providers (contractors, plumbers, electricians, painters, carpenters) bid for the work.

Once the customer picks a provider, the platform handles the rest end-to-end: real-time chat over MQTT, project tracking with progress milestones, in-app payments via HyperPay (MADA, Visa, Apple Pay), and Google Maps integration so the provider knows exactly where they're going. Everything is fully bilingual Arabic + English with native iOS and Android apps.

What customers use it for

  • Renovations — kitchens, bathrooms, full apartment refits, room additions
  • Repairs — plumbing leaks, AC issues, electrical faults, broken fixtures
  • Skilled trades — tile work, painting, carpentry, gypsum, marble work
  • Maintenance — recurring cleaning, AC servicing, garden upkeep
  • Small tasks — TV mounting, furniture assembly, hanging shelves

Key features

Verified providersBackground-checked contractors only — no walk-ins
Real-time chatMQTT-backed messaging between customer + provider
Project trackingMilestones, progress photos, status updates
HyperPay checkoutMADA, Visa, Apple Pay — Saudi-friendly
Google MapsProvider gets exact pin to your address
Bilingual UIArabic + English, with full RTL support
Phone OTP + Face IDQuick secure auth without passwords
Push notificationsFirebase FCM keeps both sides in the loop

Tech stack

SwiftUI (iOS 18.6+) Android Native Spring Boot MQTT Firebase Firestore Firebase FCM HyperPay Google Maps SDK Mixpanel

App — AI job hunter for Egypt

app.com
app.com — live preview

About

"App" is Egyptian Arabic for "there's no work / I'm jobless". It's an end-to-end job-hunting platform for Egyptian students and graduates — the goal is to take someone from "I have a CV and no plan" to "I've applied to ten real jobs this week" without leaving the app.

Egyptian graduates face a job market that doesn't behave like the one their CVs were written for. Recruiters here screen for different signals. Job boards (LinkedIn, Wuzzuf, Bayt) don't talk to each other. Career fairs at GUC, Cairo Uni, AUC dump 60+ booths on students in one day with QR codes that get lost. App fixes all of that.

The 7 things it does

CV vibe checkAI scores your CV out of 100 with sub-scores (clarity, proof, market-fit, formatting); rewrites weak bullets — Arabic or English
Job radarOne search hits LinkedIn, Wuzzuf, and Bayt at once; ranks results against your CV
CV glow-up PDFATS-optimised templates with the AI rewrites baked in, section order tuned for Egyptian recruiters
Portfolio dropShareable mini-site at app.com/portfolio/your-name — comes with a QR code
Skill roadmap8-week plan with concrete courses, portfolio tasks, and weekly checkpoints tied to your target role
Job-fair captureSnap photos of every booth's QR or poster — GPT-4o vision extracts apply link, company, role into one tidy pile
GUC Career Fair pageCurated /guc-fair with every company at this year's fair — one tap opens their apply form

Tech stack

SwiftUI (iOS) Android Native Next.js (web) Node.js GPT-4o Vision Playwright (scrapers) PostgreSQL Arabic NLP
