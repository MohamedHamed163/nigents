How It Works
Four steps to deep focus
01

Open the menu bar icon

FocusFlow lives in your macOS menu bar. One click opens the timer panel.

02

Start a focus session

Hit play or pick your duration. The circular timer shows your progress in real time.

03

Pick your ambient sound

Rain, cafe, fire, birds, waves, or wind. Get in the zone instantly.

04

Take breaks automatically

When the timer ends, FocusFlow switches to break mode. Rinse and repeat.

Installation
Up and running in seconds

Quick Install

# 1. Open the DMG and drag FocusFlow to Applications
$ open FocusFlow.dmg

# 2. Remove the quarantine flag (unsigned build)
$ xattr -cr /Applications/FocusFlow.app

# 3. Ad-hoc re-sign so macOS opens it
$ codesign --force --deep --sign - /Applications/FocusFlow.app

# 4. Launch — look for the brain icon in your menu bar
$ open /Applications/FocusFlow.app

FocusFlow is a menu-bar app (LSUIElement). It won't appear in the Dock — look for the brain icon up top.
