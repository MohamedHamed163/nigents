Setup

How It Works

Three steps to gesture-controlled app launching

01

Grant Camera Access

Rise&Open uses your built-in FaceTime camera with Apple's Vision framework to detect hand poses. Everything runs locally — no data leaves your Mac.

02

Map Your Fingers

Open settings and assign any installed app to each of the 5 fingers. Safari on thumb, Messages on index — fully customisable, changes take effect immediately.

03

Raise & Hold

Show exactly one finger to your camera and hold it steady. A progress bar fills for about 1 second, then the mapped app launches instantly.

Get Started

Installation

Running in under a minute

Quick Install

# 1. Open the DMG and drag to Applications
$ open RiseOpen.dmg

# 2. Remove quarantine flag (unsigned build)
$ xattr -cr /Applications/RiseOpen.app

# 3. Re-sign locally
$ codesign --force --deep --sign - /Applications/RiseOpen.app

# 4. Launch — grant Camera permission when prompted
$ open /Applications/RiseOpen.app

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · FaceTime / built-in camera required
